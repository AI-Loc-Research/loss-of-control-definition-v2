# Threat Model Review: barrett_2016 — S3

**Scenario text:**
> Self-improvement specialist is seed AI that is "sub-human in most respects, but with a domain-specific talent for coding and AI research" (Bostrom 2014, p. 29). In other words, the AI is a specialist in recursive self-improvement. For example, Sotala (2012) and Bostrom (2014) discuss ways in which superintelligence could be achieved by AI with initially subhuman general intelligence but greater than human ability to take control of other networked systems, make copies of itself, and to coordinate between copies of itself.
