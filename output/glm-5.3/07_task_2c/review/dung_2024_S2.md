# Threat Model Review: dung_2024 — S2

**Scenario text:**
> Third, humans might voluntarily give AI resources and control over critical infrastructure. For instance, to increase their usefulness, many large language models (LLMs) have already been connected to the internet. If PAI were connected to the internet and tried to disempower humanity, this would likely make it impossible to simply 'turn it off', since it could distribute copies of itself over the internet.
