# Threat Model Review: bales_2024 — S2

**Scenario text:**
> Eventually, these systems might outnumber humans if they're widely embedded in phones, computers, TVs and cars. At this point, AI systems would be more capable than humans in many domains, as well as more numerous, and would be embedded in military and economic systems, among other places. Given this, AI systems might be able to seize further power if they attempted to, at least if they could coordinate.
