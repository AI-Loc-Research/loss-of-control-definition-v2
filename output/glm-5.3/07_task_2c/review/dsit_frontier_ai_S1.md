# Threat Model Review: dsit_frontier_ai — S1

**Scenario text:**
> As economic production becomes increasingly dependent on AI systems, the cost of maintaining or reintroducing human control will increase. Advanced AI systems may alter complex systems in ways that are hard to understand, making it hard or risky to extract them. As a result, AI systems may increasingly steer society in a direction that is at odds with its long-term interests, even without any intention by any AI developer for this to happen.
