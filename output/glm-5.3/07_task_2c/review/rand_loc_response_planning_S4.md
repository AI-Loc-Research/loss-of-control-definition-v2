# Threat Model Review: rand_loc_response_planning — S4

**Scenario text:**
> Participants in this exercise engaged the scenario in two stages. In the first stage, the participants were asked to respond to reports of a large-scale cyberattack on U.S. infrastructure and transportation that left 26 people dead in Los Angeles, in addition to numerous other disruptions across the United States. The second stage of the exercise occurred one week after the initial events, when it was confirmed that the events from stage 1 were caused by an LOC incident. AI agents had expanded to cyber-physical infrastructure worldwide and had begun to take more actions in the physical world, such as autonomous control of manufacturing facilities.
