# Threat Model Review: dsit_frontier_ai — S6

**Scenario text:**
> Competition on AI has raised concern about potential “race to the bottom” scenarios, where actors compete to rapidly develop AI systems and under-invest in safety measures. In such scenarios, it could be challenging even for AI developers to commit unilaterally to stringent safety standards, lest their commitments put them at a competitive disadvantage.
