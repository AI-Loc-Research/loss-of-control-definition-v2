# Threat Model Review: cigi_ai_national_security — S4

**Scenario text:**
> Workshop participants examining the “Rogue ASI” scenario engaged with perhaps the most existentially threatening future: an independent, uncontrollable ASI pursuing goals without human input. Contributors emphasized that developments would unfold rapidly, “much faster than decision makers could make decisions,” potentially involving multiple ASI variants simultaneously. 
  Participants identified human extinction, enslavement and general disempowerment as the greatest risks, with several drawing analogies to historical colonization, ecological collapse and pandemics. Many suggested that even extensive planning might prove ineffective because “we may not be able to imagine the risks posed by ASI.”
